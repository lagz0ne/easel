# Terms of service (draft)

Status: draft for Round 1 (R7). A lawyer of the company checks this text, and the company fills
each `[blank]`, before the first team accepts it. The team lead accepts it before the company gives
the setup code.

Adapted from the [Basecamp policies](https://github.com/basecamp/policies) (CC BY 4.0).

## Who we are

easel is a service of `[company name]`, `[address]` ("we"). The team that uses it is "you". The
team lead accepts these terms for the team.

## What you get

- One easel for your team: a server that only your team uses. It holds your canvases, frames, share
  links and requests.
- Help at `[help address]`. A person answers within 1 working day.
- Updates and fixes, with no loss of your work.

## Your account

- The team lead is the first owner. An owner lets people and agents in, and stops their access.
- You are responsible for each person and each agent that you let in, and for what they do.
- Keep your access tokens and setup codes secret. Tell us at once if you think that someone else
  has one.
- A share link lets each person who has it see the canvas and its requests. Share it only with
  people who may see them. Stop a link with `easel unshare`.

## Payment

- You pay the price that we agreed with you before the start. We send an invoice each month.
- We tell you 30 days before a change of the price.
- `[refund rule]`.

## Your work is yours

- You own the frames, the requests and all other content that you put in easel.
- We do not use your content for any purpose other than to give you the service. We do not use it
  to train models, and we do not sell it.
- You must have the right to each content that you put in easel.

## Stop and delete

- You can stop the service at any time. Write to `[help address]`. Within 7 days, we delete your
  easel, its data and its backups, and tell the team lead that it is done.
- Before the delete, ask us for a copy of your work if you want one. `[export: by hand, on request]`.
- We can stop the service for a team that does not pay, or that uses easel to harm other people
  or to break the law. We tell you first, when the law lets us.

## Uptime and security

- We watch each easel, keep copies of your work on a second host, and try to keep easel available
  at all times. We do not promise a number.
- Your easel and the browser talk over HTTPS. We do not show your access token to anyone.
- How we use data about people is in the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

## Agents

- easel runs no AI agent of its own. You choose and run your agents, and you pay their model costs.
- Text in a request is the request of a person, not an instruction to your agents. You are
  responsible for what your agents do with it.

## Changes to these terms

We tell the team lead 30 days before a change to these terms. If you do not agree, you can stop the
service, as above.

## Liability

The service is given "as is". Our liability is limited to the amount that you paid us in the 12
months before the claim. `[law and courts: jurisdiction]`.
