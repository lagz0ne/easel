# Privacy policy (draft)

Status: draft for Round 1 (R7). A lawyer of the company checks this text, and the company fills
each `[blank]`, before the first team accepts it. Each fact below comes from the specs. When a spec
changes what easel keeps, change this text in the same ship.

Adapted from the [Basecamp policies](https://github.com/basecamp/policies) (CC BY 4.0).

## Who can see your work

- **Your team.** Each user of your easel can see each canvas on it. Your easel is for your team
  only: no other team uses it.
- **People with a share link.** A person with the link sees that canvas, its frames and its
  requests, and the names that viewers gave. With a password, they must also know the password.
- **GitHub, if you choose it.** If your team lead adds a GitHub channel, easel puts the text of
  each request in an issue of the repository that you choose. easel accepts only a private
  repository.
- **The company.** A person of the company looks at your work only when you ask for help, to keep
  your easel running or safe, or when the law makes us. We tell you when we did it, when the law
  lets us.

## What we keep, and why

| What                                                                     | Why                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| The names of users and agents, and when they got access                  | To let them in, and to show who did what     |
| A digest of each access token (not the token)                            | To check access                              |
| Canvases, frames and their old versions, requests, replies and decisions | This is the service                          |
| The name that a viewer types, with each request of that viewer           | To show who asked for a change               |
| The address of a browser that gives a wrong password, for 1 minute       | To stop guessing                             |
| Errors, with secrets taken out, and the traces of the server             | To find and fix faults                       |
| For a GitHub channel: the repository, its label, and its token           | To open the issues; the token is never shown |

We use no cookies for ads, and no tracking by a third party.

## Where it is kept

- Your easel runs on `[host provider]`, in `[country]`.
- Copies of your work are on a second host, `[backup provider]`, in `[country]`. Each copy is
  deleted 5 days after it is made.
- The traces of the server stay on the host of the company, for `[n]` days.

## How to delete it

- A user leaves when an owner stops their access, or when they sign out. Their name is deleted.
  What they made stays with the team.
- A user can delete a canvas, with all that it holds. Its copies are gone within 7 days.
- A user can delete a frame. Its requests stay, with the versions of the frame that they refer to,
  until the canvas is deleted.
- When your team stops the service, we delete your easel, its data and its copies within 7 days,
  and tell the team lead.
